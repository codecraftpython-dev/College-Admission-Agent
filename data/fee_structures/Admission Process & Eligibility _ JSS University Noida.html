    ADMISSIONS

    JOIN JSS UNIVERSITY NOIDA FOR 2026-27

    Admission at JSS University is conducted through a transparent, merit-based, and student-centric process designed to uphold academic excellence and inclusivity. The University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes across diverse disciplines, with admissions granted on the basis of national-level entrance examinations, qualifying examination performance, and institutional guidelines as applicable to each programme.

    Selection Process
    Step 01

    Candidates who apply online for the admission will be shortlisted on the basis of

    • JEE Mains
    • CAT/XAT/GMAT/CMAT/MAT score (as per the eligibility)
    • Past Academic Record
    Step 02

    Only shortlisted candidates will be called for the selection process which consists of Extempore & Personal Interview (PI).

    Step 03

    The candidates would be required to upload their entrance test percentiles (CAT/XAT/GMAT/CMAT/MAT) the moment results are out.
